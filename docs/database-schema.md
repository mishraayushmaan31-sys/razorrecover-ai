# RazorRecover AI Database Schema

This database is the server-side persistence layer for RazorRecover AI. It is designed to keep all financial and operational records under strict tenant isolation, idempotent execution, and audit-friendly accounting boundaries.

## Core design principles

- Every table includes `merchantId` for tenant isolation.
- Monetary fields use `Decimal(18,2)` to avoid floating-point errors.
- Idempotency keys are enforced for create/update-sensitive financial operations.
- Soft-delete fields (`isDeleted`, `deletedAt`) are included for operational safety.
- Audit, ledger, and notification records are intentionally append-only or append-oriented.
- Frontend never receives database credentials or server-side connection strings.

## Major tables and relationships

### Merchant
The root tenant entity. Each merchant owns users, customers, orders, policies, and ledger records.

- Relationships: one-to-many to `User`, `Customer`, `Order`, `Payment`, `RecoveryOpportunity`, `Policy`, `WebhookEvent`, `RevenueLedger`, and others.
- Constraints: unique `slug`, default `mode`, and default accounting currency.

### User
A user belongs to one merchant and one role. Users can review recovery decisions and generate activity logs.

- Relationships: `Merchant` and `Role`.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, email)`.
- Use cases: admin operators, auditors, support staff.

### Role
Defines authorization buckets for each merchant.

- Relationships: `Merchant` and `User`.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, name)`.

### Customer
Represents the payer or end customer tied to the merchant’s transactions.

- Relationships: `Merchant`, `Transaction`, `Order`, `Payment`, `RecoveryOpportunity`, `Incident`, and risk records.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, externalId)`.

### Transaction
Represents a financial transaction in the merchant lifecycle.

- Relationships: belongs to `Merchant` and `Customer`, optional `Order`, and can have many `Payment` and `RevenueLedger` entries.
- Constraints: amount must be stored as a decimal, and idempotency keys are unique per merchant.

### Payment
Represents a concrete payment attempt or successful charge from a provider.

- Relationships: to `Merchant`, `Customer`, `Order`, and `Transaction`.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, provider, providerPaymentId)` and `(merchantId, idempotencyKey)`.

### Order
Represents the merchant order being paid or recovered.

- Relationships: to `Customer`, `Merchant`, multiple `Payment` rows, payment attempts, and associated transactions.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, orderNumber)`.

### PaymentAttempt
Tracks each provider/payment lifecycle attempt with retry and deduplication context.

- Relationships: to `Merchant`, `Order`, and `Payment`.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, orderId, attemptNumber)` and `(merchantId, idempotencyKey)`.

### RecoveryOpportunity
A revenue recovery opportunity identified from a payment failure or risk pattern.

- Relationships: to `Merchant`, `Customer`, `Payment`, `Order`, `Transaction`, `Incident`, `RecoveryStrategy`, `RecoveryAction`, and `AIRecommendation`.
- Constraints: idempotency keys and risk scoring safeguards.

### RecoveryStrategy
A proposed execution strategy for a recovery opportunity.

- Relationships: to `RecoveryOpportunity` and `RecoveryAction`.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, opportunityId, name)`.

### RecoveryAction
The actual operational step executed or queued for recovery.

- Relationships: `RecoveryOpportunity`, `RecoveryStrategy`, `User`.
- Constraints: idempotency key unique per merchant to ensure safe retries.

### RecoveryExperiment
Provides a structured A/B or variant testing area for recovery workflows.

- Relationships: `Merchant`, `User`, and many `ExperimentVariant` rows.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, name)`.

### ExperimentVariant
A specific variant inside a recovery experiment.

- Relationships: to `RecoveryExperiment` and `Merchant`.
- Constraints: unique `(experimentId, name)`.

### Incident
Business or payment incident record for risk investigation and operational visibility.

- Relationships: to `Merchant`, `Customer`, and `RecoveryOpportunity`.
- Constraints: severity and lifecycle tracking via `IncidentStatus` and `RiskLevel`.

### RiskAssessment
Stores risk scoring results and supporting context for a customer, payment, or transaction.

- Relationships: to `Merchant`, `Customer`, `Payment`, and `Transaction`.
- Constraints: index on merchant, level, and payment.

### AIRecommendation
Recommendation generated by an AI model for a recovery opportunity or risk context.

- Relationships: to `Merchant`, `RecoveryOpportunity`, and `User`.
- Constraints: structured status and evidence `details`/`metadata` payloads.

### Policy
Merchant or global rules for approval and risk gating.

- Relationships: to `Merchant` and `PolicyEvaluation`.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, name, version)`.

### PolicyEvaluation
Stores each time a policy was evaluated against a resource.

- Relationships: to `Policy` and `Merchant`.
- Constraints: resource-level indexing to support review and investigation.

### HumanReview
Tracks approval or rejection decisions from a human reviewer.

- Relationships: to `Merchant` and `User`.
- Constraints: reviewer status and resource-target indexes.

### AuditLog
Append-only record of creation, update, approve, reject, and execute events.

- Relationships: `Merchant` and optional `User`.
- Purpose: compliance and historical tracing.

### WebhookEvent
Stores provider webhook payloads and their verification state.

- Relationships: `Merchant`.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, dedupeKey)` to prevent duplicate processing.

### RevenueLedger
The financial accounting ledger for recovered revenue, refunds, and adjustments.

- Relationships: to `Merchant`, `Transaction`, `Payment`, and `RecoveryOpportunity`.
- Constraints: amount and direction are used to keep posting accurate and auditable.

### MerchantMemory
AI memory data stored per merchant to maintain context without exposing direct secrets.

- Relationships: `Merchant`.
- Constraints: unique `(merchantId, key)`.

### Notification
Operational notifications sent to users or merchants.

- Relationships: `Merchant` and optional `User`.
- Constraints: status and creation-time indexing for backlog and delivery checks.

## Financial safety guarantees

- Amounts are stored as `DECIMAL(18,2)`.
- `toMoney()` and `moneyToCents()` helpers normalize values and prevent floating-point drift.
- `idempotencyKey` is enforced where retries must be safe.
- `RevenueLedger` is the canonical accounting record for financial movement.

## Local setup note

The frontend must not receive any database credentials. This database configuration remains server-side only and is expected to live in a private environment file such as `.env` or a deployment secret store.
